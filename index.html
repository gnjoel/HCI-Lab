Mission Briefing

Welcome to HCI Lab 🚀

You are about to conduct a real usability evaluation on AIU-Mart — a simulated e-commerce website with intentional design flaws. Your group will work through two phases: Heuristic Evaluation and Think-Aloud Protocol.

📋

Phase 1 — Heuristic Evaluation

Evaluate AIU-Mart against Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics. Rate the severity of any violations you find (0 = no problem, 4 = critical). Add notes for each heuristic.

🎙️

Phase 2 — Think-Aloud Protocol

One group member acts as the user and verbalizes their thoughts while completing 4 tasks on AIU-Mart. Others observe and record findings.

👥

Group Roles

Member 1: Participant (user) — performs tasks and thinks aloud
Member 2: Facilitator — reads tasks, prompts
Member 3: Observer — records findings
